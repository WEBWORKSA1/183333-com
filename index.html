FAQ

Quick answers

Is the 183-day rule the same in every country?

No. Many countries use 183 days, but the window differs (calendar year, fiscal year, or any rolling 12 months), some use 180 or 182 days, and several have no day test at all. Most also apply home, family and economic-interest tests.

If I stay under 183 days everywhere, am I tax-free?

Almost never. Your home country usually keeps taxing you until you break residency under its own rules, and some countries can make you resident on far fewer days through ties tests. US citizens are taxed wherever they live.

Does part of a day count as a day?

In many systems, yes — any part of a day counts. The UK generally counts days on which you are present at midnight. Always check the specific rule; our tracker lets you switch between counting methods.

Is my travel data uploaded anywhere?

No. The Day Tracker runs entirely in your browser and stores trips in local storage on your device. Export a CSV regularly as your backup.

Can you give me personal tax advice?

We publish education and tools, not advice. If your situation is complex, use the free matching form and we will connect you with an independent professional.
