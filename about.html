About 183333

183333.com is an independent educational project for people whose lives cross borders. We build free, private tools for counting days and testing residency rules, and we write plain-English guides that point back to the law.

Why the name

The domain reads as 18 · 3333, and we read it as 183 · 3 · 3 · 3: the 183-day threshold used by many tax systems, a three-year look-back, three residency tests, and three proofs for every stay. The number is our domain and our mnemonic — not a claim to any trademark, and not a reference to any official programme.

What we believe

  • Tools before opinions. Every calculator shows how it reached its answer.
  • Sources over hearsay. Every country summary links to the official authority.
  • Privacy by default. Your trips stay in your browser.
  • Independence. Advertisers and sponsors never edit rules or calculator logic.

How we keep content accurate

Country summaries are reviewed on a rolling schedule and dated. Readers can report outdated rules, and corrections are prioritised. Our guides are educational — for advice, use our free matching service to reach a qualified professional.

How we are funded

Through Google AdSense advertising, labelled sponsorships, referral fees from independent professionals, and reader support.
