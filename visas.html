Digital Nomad & Remote-Work Visas

Long-stay options for remote workers, with each country's tax-residency test shown alongside — because a visa that lets you stay a year can also make you a tax resident. Income thresholds and fees change often; follow the official link for current requirements.

Before you apply: check (1) whether the permit itself changes your tax residency, (2) whether the country taxes nomad income, (3) whether your home country still taxes you, and (4) whether a treaty exists between the two.
